# Individual community directory entries

Owner instruction, October 26, 2026: give individually identified peoples, bands, communities, tribal governments and historical groups their own directory cards as they are encountered. A brief queued entry is enough initially; full history and source collection can follow later.

Verify the name and relationship before publishing the card. State the entry’s kind, broader community relationship, historical or present scope, supporting identification source and what remains uncertain. Do not infer membership or descent merely from a place-name, language or neighboring community.

Genuine alternate names may have their own cards, clearly labelled as alternate-name reading routes with links to a shared collection. This preserves discoverability without implying an additional distinct nation. A named government and its historically identified constituent bands can have separate cards, with their differences and relationships explained.

Mark initial shells **Queued for research**. Identification references are supporting-only, separate from substantive history checks, adequacy, research completion and community review. A brief name check does not trigger a deep-dive completion or visual-completion claim. Neutral card decoration may be used while a queued entry awaits research; the standing authentic-picture requirement applies when substantive research is published.

Retain all existing access points and stable routes. Document verified geographic connections before adding map links; leave unsupported places or identities pending. Directory totals count access points and overlapping names, rather than distinct nations.

When normal source work encounters another verified individual group, add a scoped queued card without launching a large discovery project. Preserve the standing substantive research order: Texas → Idaho → Minnesota → Blackfeet Tribe in Montana → Michigan.
