## Inspections and scope

1. W8banaki, **Histoire de la Nation**, https://gcnwa.com/histoire-de-la-nation/ . Read substantive naming/territory, colonial rivalries, continued hunting/fishing, guiding, forestry and restrictions. Do not present all ancient regional archaeology as an exact ethnic label, use the numerical ancient population estimates, or confuse broad historical labels with present governments. 2. Québec, **Abenaki**, updated May 22, 2025, https://www.quebec.ca/en/government/quebec-at-a-glance/first-nations-and-inuit/profile-of-the-nations/abenakis . Read location, language, basketry, economy. Population/speaker figures not used. The province’s “Algonquin linguistic family” wording is imprecise; the Atlas does not convert Abenaki into the Algonquin people. Museum’s own 1965 founding statement preferred over potentially confusing provincial historical-society wording.
3. **Pilaskw May 2022**, https://caodanak.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/PILASKW_-Juin-2022_ENG.pdf . Downloaded and extracted actual ten-page PDF into `work/abenaki-evidence/Pilaskw-2022.*`. Read pp. 1–2 especially chief’s governance priorities and April29 UVM event report. Named contributors Daniel G. Nolett, Suzie O’Bomsawin, Jacques T. Watso and Mali-Agat Obomsawin. Basket-booth photographs and historical research support travel/history points. The strong criticism of four Vermont-recognized groups is an Odanak position; do not state it as the Atlas’s adjudication or let one issue settle a complex dispute. May cover date retained despite June upload path.
4. **Black ash consultation report**, March31,2022, https://gcnwa.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Rapport_Frene_noir_2021-2022.pdf . Read actual extracted PDF summary, community consultation, inventory methods and discussion (printed5–6,13–25). Relates livelihood, women’s work, knowledge transmission, proposed species-listing concerns, emerald ash borer and forest inventories. Proposed regulatory effects are the participants’ dated concern, not a statement of present law. Avoid publishing detailed mapped harvesting locations. Count one whole report, not its bibliography or maps separately.
5. **Joseph Laurent, New Familiar Abenakis and English Dialogues (1884)**. Open Library edition API resolved the scan to `newfamiliaraben01laurgoog`, which was then verified through Internet Archive metadata and actual public-domain OCR download. Text at `work/abenaki-evidence/Laurent-1884.txt`. Read title pages, preface printed5–6 and opening alphabet/pronunciation material. Crucial: primary stated purpose is teaching English to young Abenaki, alongside preserving Abenaki. Do not recast it solely as an outsider’s salvage dictionary or claim the entire dictionary/etymologies read. OCR errors mean no phonetic forms are reproduced. Date1884 supported by original edition/title/registration despite one OCR title date rendered1881.
