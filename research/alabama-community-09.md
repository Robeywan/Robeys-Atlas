## Integration cautions and disagreements

- **People and government:** Alabama is not synonymous with all Coushatta people or with a single present government. Article II explicitly identifies the joint Texas legal unit; the separate Alabama-Quassarte Tribal Town needs its own sources.
- **Chief chronology:** Chief History gives Donnis Battise's election as September 2020 and inauguration as January 2020, an internal reversal. Its last Millie Williams entry omits the later death documented by the dated tribute. The page also gives a Herbert Johnson Sr. death date differing from the Tribe's obituary listing. Do not repair those dates by guessing or present that list as current.
- **Dates and independence:** Later web additions cannot all be assigned a 2014 publication date. Howard Martin's programme and related Handbook biographies may share underlying work; do not count manifestations as independent corroboration. The constitutional cover separates 1971 adoption from 2023 consolidation.
- **Transportation status/data:** April-dated document includes a partly prospective process schedule through June; the host later calls it a final draft. Several emphasis pages still contain image placeholders. Some data prose and table percentages differ. Proposed prose repeats no rates, current totals, construction-completion claim or verified adoption claim.
- **Regional mapping:** These works support Texas as the joint government's community setting and Alabama as an origin association in its history. They do not produce reservation polygons or present-day land-title boundaries. No town pilot or geometry is supplied.
