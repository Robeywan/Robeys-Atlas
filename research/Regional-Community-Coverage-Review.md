The Texas government list expressly names Ysleta del Sur Pueblo. Its distinct reading access point was verified through the Pueblo’s own publications and the 1987 federal Restoration Act; it is not a substitute for New Mexico’s Isleta Pueblo or a broad Tiwa/Pueblo profile. Texas consultation-interest lists do not alone establish historical residence. Dakota now has an initial Minnesota-focused source pass: its profile covers community evidence from Lower Sioux, Prairie Island, Shakopee Mdewakanton and Upper Sioux, but it does not cover all Dakota communities elsewhere. Minnesota’s seven Ojibwe governments need more than a generic Ojibwe profile to achieve community-level coverage. Idaho joint governments require constituent-peoples distinctions.

Next regional research: review Minnesota’s distinct Ojibwe governments through community-specific sources, then continue Idaho. The existing regional inventories remain finding aids, not exhaustive histories. Preserve current profiles and obtain additional community evidence before any identity or map expansion.
