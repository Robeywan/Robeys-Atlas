## Scope and exclusions

- All 14 counted sources have substantive inspected content. No duplicate mirror, bare homepage, bibliography entry, or search-result page is counted.
- The original Missouri D-PLACE-style compilation was removed from the reading set in favor of more specific inspected research. It was not needed for this synthesis.
- The Heritage **Post-Contact Beothuk History** original was inspected but not counted. Several broad claims and causal formulations require newer scrutiny; the new profile relies on the narrower archaeology, Gilbert, and contemporary attributed perspectives instead.
- TRU’s open textbook chapter **Two Models of Commercial and Diplomatic Encounters—Wabanaki and Beothuk** was inspected as a useful guide to better sources, not added to inflate the count.
- `beothuk.ca` language-lesson search content asserted a Chinese/Old Norse/Gaelic creole; excluded as unsupported and inconsistent with the documented vocabulary scholarship. No living claimant’s status is decided from that exclusion.
- Chief Mi’sel Joe’s 2018 interview and Beverley Diamond’s Santu Toney chapter were found through citations but not yet read directly. They remain research priorities and are not counted. Current ancestry claims are not resolved by genetic percentages or unverified web assertions.
- No human-remains images were reproduced, and no ceremonial reconstruction was generated. Future visual design needs separate contextual and rights review.

## Method and limits

The synthesis distinguishes archaeological observation, scholarly inference, colonial documentation, Mi’kmaw oral-history perspectives, maternal-line genetics, institutional practice, and membership. Existing sources cannot settle every genealogy, classification, or present repatriation outcome. Search headlines about living descendants are not substituted for the underlying genetics papers. Short paraphrases and annotations remain within per-source limits; no extended direct quotation is used.

The PDF skill was applied read-only. Direct retrieval of public Heritage HTML used a temporary Python TLS context after the bundled Python certificate store rejected the site; no credentials or permanent security settings were involved. Local extraction snapshots and the public Qalipu/Carr PDFs are in `work/beothuk-evidence/` for audit, not website media assets.
