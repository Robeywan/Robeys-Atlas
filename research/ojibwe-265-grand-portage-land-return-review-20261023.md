# Ojibwe source review: Grand Portage Band land return

**Reviewed:** October 23, 2026  
**Profile:** Ojibwe (original access point 265), Minnesota-focused reading path  
**Source:** Grand Portage Band of Lake Superior Chippewa, [“Grand Portage Band Regains Historic Tribal Land”](https://www.grandportageband.com/grand-portage-band-regains-historic-tribal-land/), March 16, 2026.

## Inspection and use

Read the full public Band news release. It reports that a February 16, 2026 acquisition returned three parcels totaling about 87 acres to Band ownership through a partnership with the Lloyd K. Johnson Foundation and Minnesota Land Trust. The release distinguishes three ways the Band says the parcels left Band ownership: a treaty cession, a transfer into state school-trust lands, and allotment. The article quotes Band leadership and the partner representatives.

The source is added as one substantive inspected Tribal publication in the “Treaties, homelands, and changing land tenure” section. Its present-day action is attributed to the Band. The treaty, title chain, tax records, and allotment documents underlying the release’s historical account were not independently inspected. The Band’s broader descriptions of allotment policy remain attributed rather than presented as a complete neutral history. The link to map/images was not opened; no map, property coordinates, or image was added.
