## The shared design

- A full-page photographic collage creates the visual setting, continuing behind the reading area as visitors scroll.
- An opening arrangement of overlapping photographs introduces the page. Vary the arrangement and number of images to suit the available material.
- Warm, quiet colors connect the photographs. Neutral textures or gradients can fill gaps and edges.
- Cream reading panels keep introductions, facts, navigation, citations and source cards clear against the backdrop.
- Keep labels discreet within the visual arrangement when needed. Provide full artist/photographer, archive, source and reuse credits in an accessible expandable section.
- Adapt the collage for small screens, retain clear navigation and keyboard access, supply image descriptions, and keep the page usable when printing.

## What changes for each people or community

Select authentic, reusable photographs connected specifically to the profile: basketry, artwork, food, music, clothing, people, landscapes or publicly shared cultural life. The layout is shared; the photographs and cultural identity are chosen individually. Do not transfer Apache objects, designs or attribution to another people.

Record the subject, maker or photographer, date, community scope, archive, source URL, rights, actual inspection scope and any uncertain attribution. Keep broad umbrella entries distinct from particular nations and governments. Preserve uncertainty where a source says “probably” or does not identify a maker or specific community.

The owner permits optional AI-generated visual filler around the authentic photographs. Keep it decorative and distinguishable from documentary material; do not fabricate historical photographs, objects or culturally authoritative designs. The approved Apache reference uses authentic photographs and neutral CSS gradients, with no generated imagery.
