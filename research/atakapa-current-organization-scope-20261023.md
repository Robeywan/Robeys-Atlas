# Atakapa / Ishak — present-day organization and roster wording

Reviewed October 23, 2026 for the Texas-focused Atakapa profile. This is a bounded check of two short public records; it adds community-scope context but does not increase the substantive-source count.

## Records inspected

- **Louisiana Attakapas Eagle Band — homepage and self-description** ([page](https://www.louisianaattakapaseagletribe.com/)). The complete visible homepage was read, including its identification as “Louisiana Attakapas Eagle Band of the Four Winds Tribe Louisiana Cherokee Confederation,” its four-band list, its statement that the Calcasieu/Eagle band is recognized by Louisiana, its separately credited historical notes, and the footer. The page’s historical assertions do not provide citations and are not carried into the historical synthesis. The linked images, council page, newsletters, and any recognition instrument were not reviewed. This is retained as a supporting self-description, outside the substantive count.
- **Federally and State-Recognized Tribes**, Office of the Governor of Louisiana ([roster](https://gov.louisiana.gov/federal-and-state-recognized-tribes)). The complete visible roster was read. It lists “Four Winds Cherokee” under “State Recognized Tribes”; it does not separately list “Louisiana Attakapas Eagle Band.” The website’s longer “Four Winds Tribe Louisiana Cherokee Confederation” wording and the roster’s “Four Winds Cherokee” wording are similar but not identical. These pages alone do not establish whether the roster entry covers the Band. The underlying applications or administrative decisions were not reviewed. This short roster is supporting context, not a substantive history.

## Use and limits

The public profile records each source’s exact wording, keeps the Band’s self-description attributed, and avoids converting the roster into a recognition determination. It does not infer that the Band and the Atakapa-Ishak Nation are one organization, nor that a historical group label settles present identity. Neither record supplies independent evidence for colonial-era claims. No contact was made and no map was added.
