## Proposed synthesis (source-scoped)

Fond du Lac’s 2019–2022 assessment connects food access, manoomin, gardens and community education. It describes Gitigaaning as a community farm and training site, with demonstration gardens and a kitchen/cannery. These are the Band’s dated program descriptions, not verified current operating schedules or evidence of outcomes. The report’s survey ran from December 2018 to May 2019. Most analysis used 223 responses from Band members, descendants or elders, drawn from 342 total responses; some questions used broader respondent groups. The report explicitly limits its representativeness. Its methodology describes voluntary online and paper participation, so results should not be generalized to every Band member or other Ojibwe governments.
