## Limits and checks

- Nine sections distinguish 1599 destruction from regional 1680 uprising and explicitly leave local revolt/reconquest reconstruction incomplete. Do not silently fill it from the rejected NPS numerical narrative.
- Water testimony describes an advocated 2024 remedy. It does **not** claim a law enacted, settlement effective, infrastructure complete, or present officeholders.
- Land table has mixed classes and older entries; no current area number or boundary map inferred.
- Community authority is central to language law, archives, restoration, and seed rematriation. No permission implied from public access.
- Every included source is cited at least once. All twelve have `contentReviewedAt: 2026-10-19`; no duplicate URL, bare homepage, empty program placeholder, unseen bibliography title, or duplicate mirror counts toward the minimum.
- Relationships: Keres is a linguistic relationship and Pueblo a regional umbrella, not one shared government. Laguna is a separate Pueblo linked by the Rio San José context.

## Optional additions inspected after the main draft

Saved separately in `work/a-additions-04.json`; existing draft left unchanged.

- **William J. Robinson, Tree-Ring Studies of the Pueblo de Acoma: A Preliminary Report (1987), University of Arizona Laboratory of Tree-Ring Research.** https://ltrr.arizona.edu/sites/ltrr.arizona.edu/files/bibliodocs/Robinson_Tree-Ring%20Studies%20of%20the%20Pueblo%20de%20Acoma_1987.pdf . Web open failed, but direct urllib retrieved the actual 39-page PDF. Read printed pp. 1–10 and dating-symbol key p. 11. Introduction describes 1986–1987 collaboration around the Sky City Rehabilitation Project and a meeting with Acoma homeowners and representatives, including their questions about sampling impacts. Methods and results distinguish construction, repairs, missing outer rings, and limited sampling. Useful for evidence-based architecture/history without treating 1640s cutting dates as the origin of the people or whole village. Its few paragraphs of 1599/1680 history are background synthesis, not a critical trial edition. Detailed household-room catalog is not reproduced or mapped in the profile. Some last catalogue pages were visible during inspection; no named household/room information retained in the additions.
- **Christine Pasqual Sims, August 22, 2018 Senate testimony in S. Hrg. 115–367.** https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-115shrg32539/pdf/CHRG-115shrg32539.pdf . Actual 88-page hearing PDF. Adds an Acoma scholar’s own account of teacher training and community collaboration, English-centered school policies, early childhood instruction, and tribal authority. Important scope distinction: named immersion programs in Cochiti and Jemez are not Acoma programs; unnamed communities must remain unnamed. Program counts/needs are dated 2018. Not all hearing witnesses or appendix material were read.
