Audited 2026-10-20. Read `work/akateko-language-research-08.json` and the language cards and cited paragraphs actually retained in `work/akateko-integrated-08.json`. No main-site edits made.

## Result

No material correction required. The four substantial Akateko works support their present annotations and synthesis. They are distinct publications with different purposes, although they belong to a shared institutional research lineage and should not be represented as four independent field surveys. ALISA is correctly retained as supporting evidence outside the Akateko minimum.

## Actual original-source checks

- **2024 normative grammar** — Independently checked the actual 550-page PDF, especially PDF2,7–15,505–506,544 (printed pages where numbered: 3–11,501–502,540). The title/credit matter dates the document to 2024 despite the 2026 URL directory. The letter to readers explicitly separates shared writing conventions from changing speech and permits revision. Later regional examples support the account of comparing pronunciations before selecting written forms. The conclusion recommends dissemination; it does not establish universal adoption. The introduction groups Akateko with Q’anjob’al, Chuj and Popti’. These are sufficient direct grounds for both integrated paragraphs. This audit did not re-read all 550 pages or claim to reproduce the original researcher's wider selected-page inspection.
- **2013 descriptive revision** — Independently checked PDF1–3,13–14,120–122,205–206 of the actual 213-page file. The opening identifies a 2013 review/update, rather than a 2025 publication. Credit matter supports the named researchers and revision reviewer. The introduction describes fieldwork and four review workshops across San Miguel Acatán and San Rafael La Independencia. It explicitly leaves the precise classification of the observed differences unresolved and distinguishes description of speech at a particular time from writing rules. The classifier analysis and regional vocabulary tables are substantive actual contents. The integrated paragraph accurately preserves those limits and does not adopt dated examples as present universal usage. This is a different document and analytical purpose from the 2024 normative grammar, not an additional mirror of it.
- **Kukuy Ti’e Akateko teaching manual** — Independently checked actual PDF1,6–10,12,49–50 of the 91-page file. The 2011 date is tied to Miguel Angel Barillas Felipe's preparation credit; a separate publication date is not established. Spanish instructions explicitly set out listening/speaking and reading/writing aims. Actual lessons include teacher-led sound practice and Spanish glosses; the later inspected pages provide writing spaces. The current paragraph accurately describes a developed teaching approach without claiming present classroom adoption, outcomes, or an independent pronunciation/audio evaluation.
- **2019 toponymy study** — Independently checked actual PDF1–2,9–10,16,34,213 of the 217-page file. Credit matter dates the work to 2019, not its 2025 upload directory. The objective refers to updating the earlier 2003 publication. An actual entry demonstrates the combination of local description and morphological analysis, and the conclusion credits knowledgeable older community members. The integrated synthesis retains attribution rather than treating an etymology as uncontested fact. It copies no map geometry, sensitive site directions, or dated settlement counts.
- **Awakateko ALISA primary page** — Independently opened the live page at <https://alisa.aldelim.org/> and checked its introduction and §§4–5,11–12. Section 5 places Awakateko in the Mamean group and locates its original core in Aguacatán; §11 identifies the Comunidad Lingüística Awakateka's authorship and academic collaboration. Thus the Akateko/Awakateko distinction rests on the two actual primary language resources. The integrated page does not adopt ALISA's population figures or claim an audio/corpus inspection. Its supporting card is explicitly `countsTowardMinimum: false`. No audio, map, image, or corpus content is reproduced.
