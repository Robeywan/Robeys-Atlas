Its 1990 speaker/demographic figures are historical and excluded from proposed contemporary prose.
- Acknowledgments ix recognizes Lower, Central, Western, and Mentasta contributors, including Chief Jim McKinley, Fannie Shtienfield, Andy Brown, Martha Jackson, Fred Ewan, Markle Ewan Sr., Katie and Fred John Sr., Henry Peters, and Jake Tansy; it gives special credit to Mildred Buck and Ruth Johns for reviewing drafts. The contribution is not presented as one linguist's unaided work.

- p. 20 uses four regional dialect labels: Lower, Central, Western, and Mentasta. “Upper” appears as a map equivalent to Mentasta in that work. It explicitly includes some Chistochina people in Central. This is more precise than automatically equating all Chistochina speech with Mentasta. The source's grouping is reported as its own framework, not a universal contemporary census of varieties.
