# Review notes: original entries 1–8

Reviewed October 19, 2026. Seven expanded profiles; Abihka remains a reviewed source guide.

- Abenaki: community museum, Odanak newspapers and Québec nation profile. Pilaskw masthead identifies the 2022 issue as May despite June folder/file naming. Profile emphasizes Odanak and Wôlinak and flags broader coverage gaps.
- Abihka: Swanton Bulletin 73 (1922), Muscogee Nation IMLS proposal (2019), and NPS context. Historical term scope varies; contemporary community-specific continuity evidence is still needed. Smithsonian and IMLS files sometimes block automated fetch; indexed content was inspected.
- Absentee Shawnee: tribe-owned health system, official government, January 2026 newspaper, and related Shawnee governments. Separate governments remain distinct. No undated population estimates reused.
- Acoma: own identity, rebuilding, preservation office and language program pages. Oral history distinguished from archaeology; language project's restricted material is not reproduced. Colonial violence and land/water history remain coverage gaps.
- Achumawi: current Pit River constitution/history and Berkeley language guide/catalogue. Rejected old Bancroft PDF URL that redirects to a generic library page. Hokan described as hypothetical. Hewisedawi history explicitly band-specific.
- Adai: community account/programs, dated TSHA entry, Louisiana historic-site page, regional archaeological interpretation. Adaizan reconstruction is attributed; no claim that classification disputes or legal recognition are settled.
- Ahtna: intertribal commission, Native corporation, UAF and 2024 collaborative NPS study. Village governments, corporation and consortium distinguished. UAF's undated speaker counts omitted.
- Akateko: INPI, INALI and Comunidad Lingüística Akateka/ALMG toponymy volume. ALMG title and contents inspected directly; publication is 2019 despite 2025 file path. INPI/INALI focus is Mexico. Distinct from Awakateko.

All section source IDs validated during build. Profiles are independent syntheses and have not been reviewed by the communities.
