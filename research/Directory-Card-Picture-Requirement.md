# Picture required with each researched directory entry

Owner reaffirmed October 25, 2026: every researched tribe or nation should have a picture on its directory card. A colored pattern or letter-only design does not satisfy this picture requirement.

For each future researched profile publication, include an authentic, individually relevant and reusable picture on its card, retain readable wording, and keep visible photographer/artist credit with source and rights links. Review its opened page at the same time. Existing researched cards without pictures are a tracked catch-up task, including source guides beyond the earlier developed-profile visual subset.

Relevant landscapes, public cultural-life photographs, identified art or archival images can be used when their connection is supported and clearly scoped. A landscape of a river or homeland is labeled as that place, not presented as a photograph of a community or proof of a territorial boundary. Do not use another community’s object, a generic cultural symbol, or generated documentary imagery to fill a gap. Preserve and disclose image changes and reuse conditions. Image work never adds to substantive research-source counts.

The audit scope is any entry with a substantive content check, plus legacy entries carrying the developed-profile label. This is a working visual-coverage rule, not a claim that those entries are fully researched or adequate. Legacy evidence gaps remain visible. Missing rights or identification stay an explicit image gap until resolved.
