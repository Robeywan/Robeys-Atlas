## Identity and language comparison

The packet consistently distinguishes GLIFWC’s particular U.S. Ojibwe member-band context from the broad Anishinaabe identity and from Ontario institutions. Menominee collaborators remain a distinct people; shared Algonquian linguistic or climate work does not make Menominee an Ojibwe synonym. Red Cliff and Lac Vieux Desert are dated, named examples.

The language packet’s **p14-s6** remains one Ojibwe People’s Dictionary collection. Its Central Southwestern scope in Minnesota,Wisconsin and Canadian border-lakes communities is explicit. Its spelling system, regional codes and contributor attribution are not universal standards for all Anishinaabe languages. No audio-listening, pronunciation validation or present speaker-total claim is made.

**an14-l2** is a separate Indigenous-authored account of an Odawa/Eastern Ojibwe documentation project. It is not the same dictionary as OPD. The author's Wiikwemkoong perspective and the different2021workshop,2025volume and2026online dates remain explicit. Selected actual paper pages do not become a complete dictionary review or an all-community usage rule. No exact narrative, paradigm or personal recording is republished.
