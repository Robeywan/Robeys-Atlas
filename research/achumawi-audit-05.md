## Root draft cross-check

- **2019 opinion:** actual NILL text independently read at the synopsis, background, procedural history and relevant lease discussion. It supports the narrow holding concerning the twenty-six unproven leases. The single proven lease was deemed capable of commercial production; actual production had not begun. The root paragraph uses the precise term “proven.” For maximal precision, its annotation could change “one productive lease” to “one proven lease”; this is a wording improvement, not a material correction to the paragraph.
- **2025 proclamation:** actual archived primary text independently read, including the signature clause. January 14, 2025 is correct, as are approximately 224,676 acres and Forest Service management. Consultation and opportunities for co-stewardship are directions, not proof of completed management arrangements. No transfer of homeland title is asserted. The draft's restraint around sensitive locations matches the primary document's own concern.
