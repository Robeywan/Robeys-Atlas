# Lipan Apache source review: Presidio cemetery stewardship

**Reviewed:** October 23, 2026  
**Profile:** Lipan Apache (original access point 185)  
**Source:** Annie Rosenthal, [“In Presidio, the effort to preserve an Indigenous cemetery is finally complete,” Marfa Public Radio](https://www.marfapublicradio.org/news/2024-04-19/in-presidio-the-effort-to-preserve-an-indigenous-cemetery-is-finally-complete), April 19, 2024.

## Inspection and use

Read the full visible article text and captions. The page describes the 2024 completion of a public site-protection and interpretation project through attributed interviews with local descendants, an anthropologist, the Lipan Apache Tribe of Texas vice-chairman, a conservation partner, and the project architect. It reports descendant participation in historical-marker interpretation and collaboration with the Texas Historical Commission. The audio player was not played; photographs were not reused.

The profile adds the article as one substantive inspected work and cites it in “Present-day institutions and public historical work.” It is local journalism and a dated case study, not a comprehensive Tribe-authored history. The synthesis is limited to stewardship and public history, omitting sensitive burial details and avoiding conclusions about all Lipan communities or the identities of every person discussed. No map association is added.
