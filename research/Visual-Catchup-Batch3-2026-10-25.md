Jean-Flantier’s Village de Manawan, June2,2017, is explicitly licensed CC BY-SA4.0 in its actual Commons image record. Original2048×1536photograph was downloaded and visually inspected. This is a dated community view, not an ancestral boundary or a depiction of every Atikamekw community. Numerical/language claims and speculative historical interpretations are excluded.
