- A recursive string scan of the homepage registry and complete published credit JSON found **no absolute local filesystem paths, user-directory paths or file URLs**. Asset paths and older download filenames are relative names, not private machine paths.

The metadata and visible credits make no blanket claim of endorsement or universal cultural representation. Their context limits remain important for any later image reuse, motif extraction or nation-specific design.
